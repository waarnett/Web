Your last choice is remembered. “Set” switches to manual mode: the entry is used every time until you press “Use location”. “Use location” switches to device mode: the device is asked at each start, and the last device fix is kept as a fallback. Longitude is required for manual entry; latitude is optional (displayed only — no row uses it yet).
Values update every second. Sidereal and solar times are computed from UT1 = UTC + DUT1 (DUT1 is a manually maintained constant).
Location: device (Geolocation API) or manual entry, whichever you chose last; failing both, the Emerald Hills default is used for calculations. Equation of time uses Meeus low-precision formulae (~5 s accuracy).
