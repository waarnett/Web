Left out: the State of Palestine and the Holy See (UN observer states, not members), together about … million people. Kosovo is not listed separately; it is counted inside Serbia.

Taiwan: the World Bank includes Taiwan in its world population total but does not list it, so its population here is the world total minus every economy the World Bank does list. The IMF lists Taiwan’s GDP directly.

Sources, loaded live each time the page opens: population from the World Bank, World Development Indicators, “Population, total” (SP.POP.TOTL), data.worldbank.org; GDP from the IMF, World Economic Outlook, GDP at current prices in U.S. dollars (NGDPD) and in PPP international dollars (PPPGDP), imf.org.
